About

Thriller is Michael Jackson's sixth studio album and the best-selling album of all time. Blending pop, funk, rock, and R&B, it won a record-breaking 8 Grammy Awards in 1984 and reshaped modern music videos with iconic tracks like "Billie Jean", "Beat It", and "Thriller".
